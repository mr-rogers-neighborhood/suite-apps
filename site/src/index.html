What Suite is

Your files stay yours

Boards, plans and budgets are ordinary files on your computer or phone. Copy them, back them up, open them offline. No account, no hosted copy, no one else holding the original.

One home for mini-apps

Canvas for boards, Planner for days, Budget for money, Help for the handbook: small apps that live in one place, with more on the way. Each one does its job and shares the same home.

Made to share

Share a board or a plan with the people you choose. It stays your file, and you decide who gets to work on it.

The same on every screen

Suite Desk runs on your computer and Suite Go on your phone, and both are built for the same mini-apps. What you learn on one feels familiar on the other.

Free and open

Suite is MIT-licensed. Read the code, change it, keep it. Nothing here is locked to us, and there is no paid tier waiting behind the door.

Desktop and phone

Suite Desk

Alpha

The desktop side of Suite: one window, a tab for each mini-app, a Home screen and a catalogue to install more. It installs and updates from the signed Flatpak remote on Linux.

Suite Go

v0

The phone side: one home for your mini-apps, with the same rule that documents are files you own. It ships as an Android APK.

Install

Install Suite Desk on Linux or Suite Go on Android. Both are early, so keep your documents somewhere you back up.

  • Release channel — the stable Suite Desk and Suite Go builds.
  • Pre-release channels — preview, nightly and slop, installed beside the stable one.
  • Channels — what each channel promises.

Suite Desk updates from its signed remote; the rolling GitHub release is the mirror.
