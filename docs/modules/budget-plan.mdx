The plan module is the fold behind the plan view. `buildLedger` turns transactions
into account and envelope balances, `monthState` closes each envelope at the month
boundary, and `targetStatus` derives a suggestion from those numbers. Nothing here
stores a total or holds a document: the functions take records and a month and
return plain data, so the plan view and the reports read the same arithmetic.

The notes below cover the rules worth stating. The ledger and month fold are tested
against a hand-computed month and a generated invariant; debt payoff adds a
fixed-rate projection over the target's payment.

## legacy/budget/packages/core/src/plan/amortization.ts#projectDebtPayoff

The target editor can say what a fixed payment does to a debt without moving money.
`projectDebtPayoff` runs the month loop in integers: add interest, take the
payment, and stop when the balance reaches zero or the horizon is reached. The rate
is basis points and every amount is minor units, so the result cannot drift through
a float.

Three rules keep the projection honest. Interest rounds half up once per month; the
final payment is the balance plus that month's interest, never the full payment;
and a payment at or below the first month's interest returns "never pays off"
instead of an endless schedule. A balance still open after 100 years is also a
failure, so the loop has a bound. The tests pin a zero-interest loan, a multi-year
loan, the half-up case, the clamped final payment and both refusal paths.

The function is a pure projection: it does not read the document, change the
target's suggestion, or model new charges and rate changes.
